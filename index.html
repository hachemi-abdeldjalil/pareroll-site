Pareroll

Pare down your camera roll. Every photo is analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Getting started

Open Pareroll, look through the short tour (or tap Skip), and tap Allow Access to Photos. You can share your whole library or only some photos. The scan starts by itself and runs on your iPhone; the home screen shows how much space you could free in each category.

What Pareroll finds

Duplicates: the same picture saved more than once, even resized or re-saved months later. Similar shots: bursts and retakes that look alike and were taken moments apart. Screenshots, blurry photos and large videos (over 50 MB by default; change the size in Settings).

Reviewing and deleting

In Duplicates and Similar shots, Pareroll marks the best photo of each group to keep and the rest to delete; tap any photo to change that, or Keep all. In the other categories, swipe left to mark a photo for deletion and right to keep it, tap Later to decide at the end, or switch to the grid. Tap any photo to see it full screen: zoom in, play a video, and keep or delete it from there. Your choices are saved as you go.

If you scanned only the newest photos (for example the newest 1,000), tap Scan the next 1,000 on the home screen to go further back without losing what was found. Deleting photos never restarts a scan.

Nothing is deleted until you tap Delete and confirm in the iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Pareroll Pro

Scanning and reviewing are always free, and you can delete up to 1 GB for free. After that, everything you mark stays saved, and Pareroll Pro deletes without limits. Pro also keeps the best of every group in one tap and lets you choose how strict "similar" is.

Pareroll Pro is $9.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 once (Lifetime). There is no weekly plan. Prices may differ by country. The yearly plan and the 1 GB free allowance come with version 1.0.2; earlier versions sell Lifetime only and include one free 1 GB clean-up, then 30 deletions a day.

The yearly plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancel or manage it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If Pro isn't worth it to you, open Settings → Pareroll Pro → Ask Apple for a Refund in the app. Apple decides refunds.

Common questions

Do I need an account? I have a new iPhone.
No account is needed. Pro belongs to your Apple Account: on another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account it turns on by itself, or tap Restore Purchases in Pareroll's Settings. Scan results stay on each iPhone.

Some photos don't appear.
Pareroll only sees the photos you shared. If you chose "Limit Access", tap Choose more photos on the home screen. The scan skips photos stored only in iCloud; if you open one full screen, the Photos app may download it so you can see it.

The space didn't come back after deleting.
iOS frees the space once Recently Deleted is emptied, or after 30 days.

Two different photos were grouped as similar.
Pro users can choose Strict in Settings. Similar photos must also have been taken within minutes of each other.
